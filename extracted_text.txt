Notes for implementation
Typeface: Inter (weights 400/500/600/700/800). Fallback stack: -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif.

Red (#EE272A) is reserved for primary buttons and attention flags only — please don’t reintroduce it on headings, logout, or chart fills.
